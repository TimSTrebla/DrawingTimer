Review sources

A sample of 20 per search set. Tap an image to reject it: it stays out of sessions on this device. Copy verdict puts your findings on the clipboard, so the search set itself can be improved.

Your own images

Pick a folder: each subfolder becomes a category, and a subfolder named after one above (Hands, Anatomy, Pastel, AI…; Dutch works too) joins it. Put n1, n2 or n3 in a folder or file name to set the difficulty. Nothing is uploaded.

About

References come from Wikimedia Commons and Wikidata, the Art Institute of Chicago and the Cleveland Museum of Art: public domain, CC0 or CC BY, with the licence and a source link under every image. Only the sets of chosen categories are loaded; results are kept on this device for three days. Each image gets a complexity score (1–3) from its set, medium and title; the shortest poses get the simplest images. Keys: space pause · ← → · G greyscale · F mirror · R grid · Esc stop.
